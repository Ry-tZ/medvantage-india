# Growth & Cold-Start Playbook (`cgo-growth-lead`)

## 1. Solving the Two-Sided Marketplace Cold Start
Traditional healthcare job boards fail because hospitals won't post without doctors, and doctors won't join an empty job board.
- **Our Unfair Wedge**: **Live Government Medical College & INI Notifications** (AIIMS, PGIMER, ESIC, State DMEs) require **zero employer sales** to populate day-1 supply.
- Every month, thousands of JR, SR, Tutor, Assistant Professor, and Medical Officer vacancies are published across 700+ Indian medical colleges in fragmented PDFs.
- By aggregating, structuring, and alerting doctors to these government openings faster and cleaner than any Telegram channel, we acquire **verified doctors at near-zero CAC** before selling B2B access to private hospitals and private medical colleges.

## 2. The 4-Step Acquisition & Verification Funnel
1. **Top-of-Funnel (Public SEO + WhatsApp Cards)**:
   - Programmatic SEO pages for every `[Specialty] + [Designation] + [State/Institute] Vacancy` (e.g., *Senior Resident General Medicine Vacancies in AIIMS & Delhi Govt Hospitals*).
   - Public visitors see the institution, specialty, total vacancies, and deadline.
2. **Activation Gate (The NMC Reg No. Hook)**:
   - To unlock **Personalized Eligibility Check (Age/Category/TEQ Match)**, **Direct Official PDF Clauses**, and **Instant WhatsApp/Email Alerts for their Specialty**, the user enters their **NMC / State Medical Council Registration Number**.
3. **Retention Loop (Career Progression Tracker)**:
   - Track the doctor's countdown from **PG Resident (3rd Year)** $\rightarrow$ **Senior Resident (1 Year)** $\rightarrow$ **Assistant Professor Eligible**, automatically shifting recommendations as their experience clock ticks up.
4. **Monetization Conversion**:
   - Once verified doctors in high-demand specialties (Radiology, OBGYN, General Medicine, Critical Care, Orthopedics, Pediatrics) are active, surface high-paying **Verified Private Hospital & Private Medical College Faculty** roles alongside government posts.
