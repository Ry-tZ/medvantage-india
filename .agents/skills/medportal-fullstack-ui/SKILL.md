# Full-Stack Website & Workday-for-Healthcare UX (`fullstack-portal-dev`)

## 1. Core Portal Experience (No NMC Verification Friction)
1. **Instant Candidate Sign-In & Profile Switcher / Builder**:
   - Candidates create/edit their profile in seconds: **Name**, **Cadre** (`Allopathic Doctor`, `Dental`, `Nursing`), **Highest Qualification** (`MBBS`, `MD/MS`, `DM/MCh`, `BDS`, `MDS`, `B.Sc Nursing`, `M.Sc Nursing`, `GNM`), **Specialty/Department**, **Years of Experience**, **Age**, **Preferred State**, and **Open to Fellowships**.
2. **"For You" Personalized Recommendations Tab**:
   - Dynamic match score ($0-100\%$) with clear match explanation badges (e.g., *"96% Match • Fits your MDS Oral Surgery + 2 yrs exp"* or *"94% Match • B.Sc Nursing + ICU Experience"*).
3. **Live Government & State DME Notification Radar**:
   - Live scraper control panel + real-time feed covering **AIIMS, PGIMER, JIPMER, ESIC, NHM, and State DMEs (Maharashtra DMER, Karnataka DME, TN MRB, UP DGME, Delhi MAIDS/DSSSB)**.
   - Includes a **"Run Live Govt/DME Scraper"** trigger that fetches and parses live recruitment data and updates the feed.
4. **Private Hospital Openings & 1-Click Direct Apply**:
   - Openings from Apollo, Fortis, Max, Manipal, Medanta, Narayana, Clove Dental, etc., across Doctors, Dentists, Nurses, and Fellowships.
   - Candidates click **"1-Click Apply with Profile"**, attach a cover note/availability, and track their application status in **"My Applications"**.
5. **Dedicated Fellowships & Super-Specialty Hub**:
   - Filterable hub for Clinical Fellowships, FNB, Post-Doc Fellowships, Dental Implantology/Maxillofacial Fellowships, and Critical Care Nursing Fellowships.
6. **Hospital & College Employer Portal ("Post Opening & Review Applicants")**:
   - Hospitals can post new openings for **Doctors, Dentists, Nurses, or Fellowships** directly in the UI and manage incoming candidate applications.
