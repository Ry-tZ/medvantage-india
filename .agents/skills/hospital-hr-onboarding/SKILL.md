# B2B Hospital & Private Medical College Onboarding (`hospital-b2b-sales`)

## 1. Target B2B Customer Segments & Pain Points

### Segment A: Private & Deemed Medical Colleges (700+ Colleges in India)
- **Acute Pain Point**: NMC conducts strict AEBAS (Aadhaar Enabled Biometric Attendance System) and faculty deficiency inspections. Private medical colleges desperately need **NMC TEQ-compliant Senior Residents, Assistant Professors, Associate Professors, and Professors** with verified teaching experience and research publications.
- **Our Pitch**: *"Filter pre-verified doctors by exact NMC TEQ eligibility (1-yr SR completed, BCBR certified, indexed publications verified) and hire faculty in 7 days instead of paying recruitment consultants 8.33%–16% commission."*

### Segment B: Corporate & Tier-2/3 Private Hospitals (Apollo, Manipal, Max, Fortis, Regional Multi-Specialty)
- **Acute Pain Point**: General job portals (Naukri/LinkedIn) are flooded with unverified CVs, non-doctors, or unqualified BAMS/BHMS applicants applying for MD/DM ICU & Consultant roles.
- **Our Pitch**: *"100% NMC/SMC Registry-Verified Specialists & Super-Specialists. Zero fake CVs. Direct 1-click verified credential packets."*

## 2. Revenue & Pricing Architecture
1. **Government Notifications**: 100% Free forever for verified doctors (our traffic & trust moat).
2. **Private Medical Colleges & Hospitals (SaaS + Hire)**:
   - **Starter Posting Tier**: Free to post 1 active verified role (to seed private supply).
   - **Pro ATS & Verified Talent Search**: Monthly subscription for HR teams to filter and message `TIER_2_VERIFIED` and `TIER_3_CREDENTIALED` doctors directly.
   - **Urgent Locum / Duty Doctor & Faculty Placement Boost**: Sponsored placement inside the specialty-matched feed and WhatsApp alert digest.
