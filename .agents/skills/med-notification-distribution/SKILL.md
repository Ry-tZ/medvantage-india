1. **PG Passing-Out Batch Loops**: Target final-year MD/MS/DNB residents 60 days before university/NBE exams—every single one needs a Senior Residency (SR-ship) or Bond/MO placement immediately after results.
2. **Resident Doctors' Associations (FORDA / FAIMA / State RDAs)**: Provide free "Live SR & Bond Vacancy Trackers" for state associations.
